Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to the questions we're asked most often.

How do online lessons work?

Lessons take place online using Microsoft Teams or Google Meet. Students receive a meeting link before each lesson.

Which subjects do you teach?

We offer tutoring in Mathematics, English, Irish, Biology, Chemistry, Physics, Business, Geography, History and Computer Science.

Which year groups do you teach?

We currently support Junior Cycle and Leaving Certificate students across Ireland.

How long is each lesson?

Standard lessons last one hour. Longer sessions can be arranged if needed.

Can I reschedule a lesson?

Yes. Please provide at least 24 hours' notice and we'll arrange another suitable time.

Do you offer homework support?

Yes. Students can receive help with homework, coursework, revision and exam preparation.

How do I book?

You can book through our Contact page or email us directly at mindoratutors@gmail.com.

How do I pay?

After your booking has been confirmed, we'll provide you with a secure payment link or invoice for online payment. Cash payments may also be available by prior arrangement.

How quickly will you reply?

We aim to reply to all enquiries within 24 hours.

Can I arrange regular weekly lessons?

Absolutely. Many students book weekly lessons throughout the school year for consistent support and progress.
